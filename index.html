Local Mail 2

New messaging tool developed by UNIMOODLE, designed to improve communication between course participants. This service allows users to send messages to other participants, using an interface and functions similar to those of webmail clients.

Community project

In the framework of the European NextGeneration funds with the UNIDIGITAL program, the universities of Valladolid, Complutense de Madrid, País Vasco/EHU, León, Salamanca, Illes Balears, Valencia, Rey Juan Carlos, La Laguna, Zaragoza, Málaga, Córdoba, Extremadura, Vigo, Las Palmas and Burgos created in October 2022 an inter-university group to develop new tools to improve virtual campuses based on Moodle.

¿Qué es Local Mail 2?

Local Mail 2 is an internal messaging plugin designed specifically for Moodle. This plugin provides an efficient solution for sending and receiving messages within the platform environment, using an intuitive interface similar to that of webmail clients. What sets Local Mail 2 apart is its focus on relevant communication, whereby messages are linked directly to the learning space in which users are enrolled, thus ensuring that communication is always relevant and focused on course or subject content.

Why use it?

Using Local Mail 2 in Moodle is an effective way to enhance communication and collaboration among course participants. By centralizing messaging on the platform, it ensures that all interaction is relevant and directly related to course content, promoting smooth and agile communication between students and instructors. With advanced features such as the creation of custom folders, filters and an enhanced advanced search tool, this plugin improves the management of communication within courses. In addition, its accessibility from any device facilitates efficient management and more cohesive and participatory learning.

Who is the target audience?

Local Mail 2 is designed for those looking for an effective solution to manage internal communication. It is ideal for students who want to interact with their peers and receive key information from their instructors, as well as for teachers who need an effective tool for sending notices, answering queries and fostering collaboration among students. In short, Local Mail 2 is an essential tool for any member of the Moodle learning community who wants to improve interaction and communication in an efficient and focused manner.
